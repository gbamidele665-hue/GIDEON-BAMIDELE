About

Gideon Bamidele is a tech enthusiast and entrepreneur passionate about technology, fashion and creativity. He is the founder of BAMSZN, an emerging fashion and streetwear brand built on individuality, confidence, ambition and the philosophy of No Off Szn — a mindset of creating your own opportunities and never waiting for the perfect season. He is continuously exploring ideas, developing skills and building at the intersection of technology, creativity and entrepreneurship.
